# Mac42 preparation — unbound, not dispatched

Derived from exact41 kit a37bea4. Original replacement and real TemporaryFile
probes, exact installed module inventory/bytes, checkpoint and900-second owner
procedure are unchanged. Source/hashes and owner/workspace/deadline remain
unbound. Manual workflow uses MESHIA_MAC42_PAIR_GRANT. No push or dispatch yet.

The observer now preserves the exact guard-decision snapshot on
fabric_mount_rename_busy: pending_kind, pending_mutation_id, pending_journal_seq,
latest_kind, latest_mutation_id, latest_journal_seq. Kinds must be put/delete/rename,
IDs canonical lowercase UUIDs, sequence values bounded nonnegative integers or
the logger's exact decimal strings. Existing bool strings remain strictly
decoded. Unknown fields, payloads, paths and secrets are excluded. Product
captures the snapshot at the guard, not by a later database reread.

Use prepare_bindings.py with final42 candidate and wheel only after final
source/proxy/Linux diagnostics pass. Older preparation documents are historical
and their bound source versions must not be reused.
